About MR Tech Automation

Engineering-driven industrial automation specialists

MR Tech Automation is a technically focused industrial automation company delivering fully custom control systems for process and discrete industries. We specialize in PLC software engineering, SCADA and HMI development, LabVIEW-based automation, industrial networking, and system commissioning.

Our approach is simple: 100% in-house engineering. No generic platforms, no vendor lock-in — only systems designed precisely for plant logic, performance, reliability, and future scalability.
