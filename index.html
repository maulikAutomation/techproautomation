About MR Tech Automation

Engineering-driven industrial automation specialists

MR Tech Automation is a technically focused industrial automation company delivering fully custom control systems for process and discrete industries. We specialize in designing PLC architectures, developing control logic, building SCADA/HMI software from scratch, and integrating industrial networks with high reliability.

Our philosophy is simple: no black-box platforms, no vendor lock-in. Every system is engineered in-house to match the exact operational logic, performance, and expansion needs of the plant.

Engineering Services

Deep technical automation capabilities

PLC Software Engineering

Structured, scalable PLC programs using IEC 61131 standards including ladder, function block, and structured text.

Custom SCADA & HMI

In-house developed visualization systems with custom graphics, alarms, trends, historian, and role-based access.

Control Panel Engineering

Electrical schematics, panel layouts, IO mapping, BOM, FAT & commissioning support.

Drives & Motion Control

VFDs, servo systems, synchronized motion, and closed-loop control applications.

Industrial Communication

Design and implementation of deterministic and redundant industrial networks.

System Commissioning

On-site testing, loop checking, performance tuning, and plant handover documentation.

Industry Solutions

Custom automation engineered for real-world industries

Manufacturing Automation

End-to-end automation for discrete and continuous manufacturing lines including conveyors, assembly, and packaging.

Pharma & FMCG

Batch processes, recipe management, traceability, and compliance-focused automation systems.

Water & Wastewater

Automation for pumping stations, STP, WTP, and distribution networks with real-time monitoring.

Energy & Utilities

Power monitoring, energy management, and substation automation solutions.

OEM Machine Automation

Custom control software and electrical design for machine builders and OEMs.

Retrofit & Upgrades

Modernization of legacy PLC, HMI, and control systems without disrupting production.
